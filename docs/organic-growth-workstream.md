# Organic Growth workstream

Product 10 establishes an ongoing, unpaid acquisition system: reviewed exercises, deterministic tools, topic hubs, and a 25-topic editorial queue.

## Editorial cadence

Every candidate moves through research, draft, evidence check, technical review, editorial review, SEO/UX review, approval, and publication. A draft is never automatically published. Quarterly review checks evidence currency, broken links, product-methodology changes, search intent, accessibility, and content decay. Material changes create a revision record.

The 25 launch topics are currently in `editorial_review`; a qualified human must complete evidence, technical, editorial, and SEO/UX gates before publication. This deliberately prevents unreviewed copy from becoming indexable.

## Measurement

Track resource type/slug, topic, CTA location, and methodology version. Never send calculator inputs or results to analytics. Review resource-to-assessment conversion, useful search impressions, completion signals, and decay—not vanity traffic alone.
