# Product 12 consumer design system

## Principles

The interface should feel calm before it feels impressive. Warm white carries reading and form surfaces; near-black frames the product; bronze marks action and progress. Customer-facing text describes decisions and outcomes rather than internal systems.

## Foundations

- Color: `#080909` canvas, `#121414` graphite surface, `#f2eee4` warm paper, `#f8f6ef` primary text, `#c9a456` bronze action, `#92753a` restrained secondary bronze.
- Type: Geist for interface and small labels; Georgia for editorial and expressive display. Display is capped at 104px desktop and 62px phone; body is 17–20px with 1.6–1.8 line height.
- Spacing: 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96/128 scale. Major sections use 96–128px desktop and 64–80px phone.
- Grid: 1180px marketing, 960px product funnel, 680px reading column. Twenty-pixel phone gutters.
- Surfaces: 4–8px radii for precise product frames, 16–20px for human interaction panels. One-pixel low-contrast borders; shadow reserved for overlays and signature reveals.
- Controls: 54px minimum input height and 48px minimum touch targets. Primary action is bronze/black; secondary is transparent with visible border and focus ring.

## Page compositions

- Homepage: proposition → personalized artifact → connected product demonstrations → real-life adaptation → trust → free value → membership → CTA.
- Assessment: single focused prompt, visible progress and section name, explanatory microcopy, large response targets, persistent back/continue controls. Twenty core inputs with conditional body-composition and limitation details.
- Blueprint: progressive signature reveal with strategy headline, training week, nutrition range, activity/recovery priorities, rationale, eight-week trajectory, and one dominant trial action.
- Tools: category-led index; purpose → minimal input panel → interpreted result → limits → related guide → assessment.
- Insights: publication front page; article uses a 680px reading measure, sticky contents on wide screens, examples, callouts, related resources, and transparent authorship.
- Exercise: demonstration and anatomy dominate above the fold; instruction, cues, mistakes, role, cautions, substitutions, and related resources follow.
- Auth/account: warm-paper focal panel, explicit stage and recovery options, no developer vocabulary.

## Responsive rules

- Small phone 360px: one column, no clipped horizontal content, persistent assessment controls, 20px gutters.
- Large phone 430px: single-column product demos with retained hierarchy.
- Tablet 768px: two-column cards where useful, assessment remains focused.
- Desktop 1280px: full product frames and supporting rails.
- Wide desktop 1600px: content remains capped; whitespace expands rather than type.

Motion is limited to 160–300ms state transitions and respects reduced-motion preferences. No content relies on animation to be understood.
