# Nutrition and supplements before and after

Nutrition calculations remain unchanged: activity/goal estimates, protein and fat ranges, flexible carbohydrates, fiber, hydration, steps, and conservative cardio. Newly generated targets now include methodology version, `NUT_STARTING_TARGET`, and linked claim IDs.

App 6A adds neutral creatine-monohydrate and caffeine guidance. Both are optional and include uncertainty, when unnecessary, health/medication cautions, and clinician-review boundaries. No branded products, medication changes, extreme diets, or automatic supplement prescriptions were added.
