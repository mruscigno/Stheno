# Training before and after

Before: deterministic starting sets, RIR, rep ranges, progression, deload, and substitutions existed in separate modules.

After: those rules remain behaviorally stable and are described by `TRN_STARTING_DOSE` and `TRN_PROGRESSION`, linked to explicit evidence claims and limitations. Novice, intermediate, time/equipment adaptation, plateau, low-recovery, concurrent-running, and pain boundaries are represented in policy tests or existing engine tests. No active program was regenerated.
