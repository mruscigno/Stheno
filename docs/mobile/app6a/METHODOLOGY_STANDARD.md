# STHENO methodology standard

STHENO uses an independent, versioned methodology. A policy is eligible only when its required inputs exist and no contraindication or safety boundary supersedes it. Every policy records its version, rule, evidence claims, confidence, output, explanation, overrides, and test fixtures in `src/modules/methodology/policies.ts`.

The evidence hierarchy favors current position stands and systematic reviews. Public work associated with Jim Stoppani, Layne Norton, and Michael Matthews is used only to compare practical programming approaches. It is not copied, treated as endorsement, or used to impersonate an expert.

Deterministic engines own prescriptions and changes. Coach may explain those outputs with member context and reviewed evidence, but cannot silently rewrite a plan. Incomplete evidence produces a maintain/no-change decision. Pain, emergency indicators, medical nutrition needs, medication management, pregnancy, and eating-disorder concerns route outside optimization.

Feature flags `STHENO_METHODOLOGY_V2` and `STHENO_METHODOLOGY_COACH` can disable additive policy metadata or Coach context without reverting the preserved engines.
