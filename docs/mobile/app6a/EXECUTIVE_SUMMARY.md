# App 6A executive summary

App 6A adds a shared, versioned coaching methodology without replacing STHENO's working deterministic engines. Training, nutrition, Coach, and monthly review now expose or consume the same policy and evidence vocabulary. Supplement guidance is bounded and optional. Five exercise-specific form-review rubrics prepare App 6B without implementing media analysis.

The release does not rewrite active plans, add infrastructure, alter the canonical exercise catalog, or change workout transaction/outbox behavior.
