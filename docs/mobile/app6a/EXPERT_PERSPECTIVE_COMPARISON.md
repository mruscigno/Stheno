# Public expert perspective comparison

| Perspective | Publicly documented emphasis | Adopted by STHENO | Not adopted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jim Stoppani | Planned manipulation of training variables and periodization | Versioned progression, explicit exercise variables, planned variation when a plateau is supported | Branded programs, advanced intensity techniques as defaults, claims of endorsement |
| Layne Norton | Progressive overload, distributing useful volume, selective rather than universal failure | Conservative progression, RIR-aware work, evidence before change | Persona, branded PHAT template, universal frequency or failure rules |
| Michael Matthews | Simple progressive overload and repeatable compound-focused programming | Beginner-friendly explanations, measurable progression, consistency-first framing | Branded programs, rigid exercise dogma, persona or endorsement |

Where perspectives disagree, STHENO uses the higher-quality research boundary and the member's constraints. Complexity is never added merely because an expert uses it.
