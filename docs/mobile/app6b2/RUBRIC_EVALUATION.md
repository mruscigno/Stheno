Only Goblet Squat could be evaluated because it was the only supported exercise with clearly STHENO-owned test footage. Romanian Deadlift, Lat Pulldown, Dumbbell Press, and Row were not sent; absence is recorded rather than substituted.

The four-frame result cited all four Goblet Squat checkpoint IDs exactly and used only supplied timestamps. It appropriately lowered confidence for knee tracking and depth, identified camera mismatch, and avoided joint angles, pain, diagnosis, and safety guarantees. Cues were actionable and bounded. Human ground truth was not supplied by a qualified coach, so accuracy is **human-reference unverified**; no aggregate accuracy percentage is claimed.
