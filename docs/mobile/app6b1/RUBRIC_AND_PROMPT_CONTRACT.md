# Rubric and prompt contract

Only these reviewed rubrics are accepted: Goblet Squat, Barbell Romanian Deadlift, Lat Pulldown, Dumbbell Bench Press, and One-Arm Dumbbell Row. The prompt supplies exercise identity, camera view, sampled timestamps, rubric version, methodology version, observable checkpoints, and limitations.

Output must identify only supplied checkpoint IDs and timestamp values. It is limited to observable evidence, three positives, three priorities, neutral cues, visibility quality, limitations, recommended camera view, and a caution. Diagnosis, pain inference, exact joint loading/angles, safety guarantees, and invented motion are prohibited. Insufficient evidence requires abstention.
