# Training methodology V1

Ruleset `2026.08-v1` supports 2–5 resistance-training days and sessions of at least 25 minutes. It resolves full-body or upper/lower-oriented structures from schedule, then selects exercises by movement intent, equipment, experience, preferences, avoidances, fatigue cost, and progression suitability.

Prescriptions include sets, goal-specific rep ranges, reps-in-reserve targets, rest, alternatives, reason codes, and engine/ruleset versions. Double progression increases reps inside the prescribed range, then raises load conservatively when all sets reach the top of the range at the target effort. Missed rep floors, repeated stalls, and fatigue flags produce hold, reduction, or deload decisions.

This is a conservative software ruleset, not medical care. Reported discomfort routes to modification and confirmation without diagnosis. The methodology needs dedicated credentialed expert review before broad public launch; source/evidence governance will expand with the library.
